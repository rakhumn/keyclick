What it does

KeyClick plays a mechanical-keyboard sound on every keypress, system-wide, from the menu bar. The sounds are real recordings of 13 different switches, plus a fully synthesized Funny Mode pack.

Privacy

KeyClick sees only that a key went down or up — a numeric key code, never the characters you type. It keeps no keystroke history: the only thing stored on disk is your settings file. All processing happens on your Mac; nothing is recorded or sent anywhere. Password fields stay invisible to all global listeners by macOS design (Secure Keyboard Entry).

Open source
